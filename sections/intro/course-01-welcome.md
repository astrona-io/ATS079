# Welcome To The Course

This course develops the official Continuous Delivery & Platform Engineering objectives for Certified Cloud Native Platform Engineering Associate (CNPA). The certification uses a multiple-choice examination. The course is independently authored and does not claim to simulate the examination, but it adds hands-on practice so that you can see each mechanism work.

Each section starts with its own introduction and learning outcomes. Each module provides goals, deep-dive reading parts, a playground (a free environment without a task), a graded lab and a final summary. Each section ends with a capstone lab and a quiz, and the course ends with a final domain quiz.

The example workloads are named after a fleet: `bridge`, `scout`, `shuttle` and `probe` in the `starfleet` namespace. The names are only labels for examples. Technical pages remain draft for review.

Read the explanations to identify the responsible component, the intended operation and the limits of each claim, then confirm what you read in the playground.
