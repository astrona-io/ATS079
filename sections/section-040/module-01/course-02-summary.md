# Summary

A GitOps-managed system includes the target runtimes, their management agents and the policies controlling sources, deployments and access. Its boundary is broader than a repository but narrower than every system it references. Bootstrap establishes the initial trusted management path, and ongoing ownership determines what agents may change. Source and target identities should have limited authority, with observable status for dependencies that remain outside the management scope.
